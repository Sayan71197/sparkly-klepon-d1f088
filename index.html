About the Workshop

Engineering microalgae for sustainable bioproduction

Microalgae are emerging as promising sustainable cell factories for producing value-added products, including biofuels, biochemicals, pharmaceuticals, pigments, nutraceuticals, and other high-value biomolecules. Advances in metabolic engineering, process engineering, omics-based approaches, metabolic flux analysis and machine learning are enabling a deeper understanding of microalgal metabolism and facilitating enhanced and sustainable bioproduction.

The integration of these approaches with life cycle assessment further supports the development of efficient and sustainable algal biorefineries and their translation toward industrial-scale production.

This five-day workshop will bring together leading researchers and experts to discuss recent advances in metabolic and process-scale engineering of microalgae through expert lectures, hands-on tutorials, and interactive discussions.

Workshop Outline

From metabolic models to sustainable processes

The programme connects computational and experimental approaches across the algal bioproduction pipeline.

01

Reconstruction of Genome-scale Metabolic Models for Algae

Systems-level reconstruction and analysis of algal metabolism.

02

Metabolic Engineering of Photosynthetic Microbes

Engineering metabolic pathways for improved bioproduction.

03

Genome Editing Tools in Microalgae

Modern genetic tools for targeted strain engineering.

04

Process Scale Engineering of Microalgae

Translating strain-level advances toward scalable cultivation and production.

05

Metabolic Flux Analysis of Photosynthetic Microbes

Quantitative understanding of intracellular metabolic fluxes.

06

Machine Learning-based Bioprocess Optimization

Data-driven prediction and optimization of algal bioprocesses.

07

Life Cycle Assessment

Assessing sustainability and environmental performance of algal biorefineries.
